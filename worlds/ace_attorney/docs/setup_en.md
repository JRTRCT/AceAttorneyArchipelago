## A Note on Game Support

My intent is to eventually support all four modern PC ports,
however initial development will be for the Apollo Justice Trilogy.

## Required Software

- [Archipelago](https://github.com/ArchipelagoMW/Archipelago/releases/latest)
- [The Ace Attorney apworld](https://github.com/JRTRCT/AceAttorneyArchipelago/releases), 
  if not bundled with your version of Archipelago

## How to Play

First, you need a room to connect to. For this, you or someone you know has to generate a game.  
This will not be explained here,
but you can check the [Archipelago Setup Guide](/tutorial/Archipelago/setup_en#generating-a-game).

You also need to have [Archipelago](https://github.com/ArchipelagoMW/Archipelago/releases/latest) installed
and the [The Ace Attorney apworld](https://github.com/JRTRCT/AceAttorneyArchipelago/releases) installed into Archipelago.

From here, connecting to your Ace Attorney slot is easy. There are two scenarios.

## Joining a Room

Once the room has started, you can launch the Ace Attorney randomizer manually.  

Open the Archipelago Launcher, and then select the Ace Attorney Client from the list.  
After a short while, the Ace Attorney client should open.  
Enter the server address at the top and click "Connect".  
Then, enter your name. If a password exists, enter the password.  
You should now be connected and able to play Ace Attorney.
